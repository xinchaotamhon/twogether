# Information Policy

Stable navigation belongs in `START_HERE.md`. Durable product and learning invariants belong in `00-Governance` and ADRs. Current status, blockers, and next work belong in `40-State`. Measurements, screenshots, query receipts, and test output belong in `50-Evidence`. Shared cards, nodes, and review logs are business data owned by the application database, not Markdown. Credentials and push subscription secrets stay outside the repository.

Raw source captures are immutable inputs. Derived cards must record source reference, transformation, author/reviewer, and uncertainty. A link or publisher claim is provenance, not automatic truth. Private learner notes are detachable and must not be copied into shared project memory.
