# Core curriculum synthesis v2

English Generative Core v1 is a complete **foundation backbone**, not a claim to contain all English. It replaces the 20-card English starter with 80 reviewable drafts while preserving the 10 React starter cards and their source boundaries.

## Ten-module shape

1. Meaning → Clause
2. Verb Architecture
3. Negation & Questions
4. Time, Aspect & Modality
5. Noun & Reference
6. Expanding the Clause
7. Information Flow
8. Sound & Listening
9. Lexicon as a Network
10. Integration & Production

Each module has eight cards: one `core_recall`, two `mechanism`, one `contrast`, one `boundary`, one `application`, and two `production` cards. This is a review unit, not a claim that every concept naturally needs the same number of cards forever.

## Learning invariant

Every module moves through the reusable ladder:

```text
principle → mechanism → nearest contrast → boundary → application → production → integration
```

A model answer must be short enough to self-grade, but the explanation and transfer cue must expose why the idea works, where it stops working, and what the learner should do in a fresh context.

## Graph invariant

The ten modules form a DAG. A module can depend on several earlier mechanisms; its screen position is not semantic truth. Card IDs `core-en-01` through `core-en-20` are preserved for continuity, revised in place, and followed by `core-en-21` through `core-en-80`.

## Promotion rule

All curriculum nodes, collections, and cards remain `draft`. The app exposes ten eight-card review slices in the `Thẻ` view, but the study shelf remains published-only. Hiệp or Hoàng must review content before any draft becomes a live study item.
